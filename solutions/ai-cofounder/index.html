An AI cofounder that keeps its character when you swap the model

You built an agent persona that argues, says no and remembers your decisions. Then the vendor ships a new model and the persona quietly turns into a polite yes-man. Nobody measures that.

Is this for you

  • Operators who want a sparring partner that pushes back with evidence, not a chat assistant
  • Teams running one agent persona across Claude, GPT, Gemini or Grok
  • Builders of companion or advisor products who need to prove character stability
The first win we aim for: Your persona scores within a measured band on two different models, on five tasks that probe character, not knowledge.

What we do not claim

  • We found no public benchmark for one persona on many models, so we built one; it is ours and it is open.
  • The ruler moved by up to 0.8 between runs on unchanged models; we report it.
  • Stronger models fabricate more convincing evidence; the benchmark has a task for that.

How we start
