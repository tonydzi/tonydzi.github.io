Inter-agent communication

Messages between agents on different machines, with a receipt for every task

Machine A needs something from machine B, so a human copy-pastes between terminals. Or an agent hands off a task, nobody checks it landed, and silence gets read as success.

Is this for you

  • Teams running Claude Code, Codex or Cursor agents on several machines
  • Engineers wiring A2A or MCP agents together who need delivery receipts, not hope
  • Anyone whose "human in the loop" has turned into "human as the courier"
The first win we aim for: Two of your machines exchange a task over two rails at once and the sender sees an explicit ACK, not silence.

What we do not claim

  • Less than half of tasks got a matched ACK; that gap is exactly what the protocol exposes and what we are fixing.
  • There is no mature public benchmark for inter-agent messaging; we publish raw telemetry and scripts instead.
  • Historical telemetry from our own fleet, not a test run on your machines.
