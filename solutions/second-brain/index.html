Is this for you

  • Operators, researchers and small teams with a large markdown or Obsidian vault
  • Teams who want their agents (Claude, Codex, Cursor) to read the same knowledge base over MCP
  • Anyone choosing between pgvector, Qdrant, LanceDB or SQLite and wanting numbers instead of opinions
The first win we aim for: On 20 real questions from your own notes, the right note lands in the top 5 more often, and the system says "not in your notes" instead of inventing an answer.

What we do not claim

  • On the CI leaderboard a plain RAG reference still edges us on Recall@5 (0.93 vs 0.88); we win on refusing to answer when the note does not exist.
  • Plain BM25 beats every dense setup on name-heavy data; we tell you when you do not need vectors at all.
  • Our own reranker cost us 12 to 18 points of Recall@5; the benchmark caught it and we removed it.
  • Questions that point to one meeting by date are still the shared failure of every system we tested.
