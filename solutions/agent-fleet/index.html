Multi-machine agents

Many machines, many agents, no human courier in the middle

A fix lands on the desktop and the laptop never hears about it. You copy-paste between terminals. "Sent" quietly becomes "done". This is what happens once you run agents on more than one machine.

Is this for you

  • Teams running Claude Code, Codex or Cursor agents on several machines
  • Engineers building inter-agent messaging (A2A, MCP) who want a protocol that has survived production
  • Anyone who needs machines to agree on routine decisions and wake a human only for risky ones
The first win we aim for: Two of your machines exchange a signed, acknowledged task without you copying anything between them.

What we do not claim

  • There is no mature public benchmark for multi-machine consensus; we publish our own telemetry and a zero-token harness.
  • The decline from 121 to 6 proposals is a finding about coordination decay, not a success metric; we publish it as such.
  • Preprint on arXiv (cs.MA) is pending endorsement; the repository copy is canonical.
