Machine consensus

Let your machines agree on routine decisions and wake you only for risky ones

Once agents run on several machines they drift apart, two of them commit conflicting changes, and every decision ends up back on your desk.

Book a 15-minute Google MeetRead the code

Is this for you

  • Teams running Claude Code, Codex or Cursor agents on several machines
  • Engineers building inter-agent messaging (A2A, MCP) who want a protocol that has survived production
  • Anyone who needs machines to agree on routine decisions and wake a human only for risky ones
The first win we aim for: A routine change is proposed, accepted and independently verified by a second machine, and a risky one waits for you.

What we do not claim

  • Proposals fell from 121 to 6 a month: a finding about coordination decay, published as such, not a success metric.
  • Historical telemetry from our own fleet; no public consensus benchmark exists.
  • Preprint on arXiv (cs.MA) is pending endorsement; the repository copy is canonical.
